The Javanese script is not an alphabet in the ordinary sense. It is a sentence — a story about two envoys, memorised by every child who learns to write.

01 — THE LEGEND

Two servants. One master. Two instructions that could not both be obeyed.

THE LESSON

Loyalty without context is dangerous.

Neither envoy betrayed anyone. Both executed faithfully. What killed them was two orders that collided, no way to verify, and no human between them at the moment it mattered.

02 — FROM STORY TO ARCHITECTURE

Every line of the verse is a design decision.

This is not decoration applied after the fact. The failure mode in the legend is precisely the failure mode of an autonomous agent with permissions — so the story became the checklist.

04 — A SISTER PROJECT

One remembers. One is sent.

Caraka and Titen are written by the same author. An envoy without memory repeats the same mistakes; memory without an envoy does no work. The legend explains why both must exist together.
